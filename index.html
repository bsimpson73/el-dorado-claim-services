FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

What information is required?

Photographs of all affected areas, room measurements or a floor-plan scan, and a brief description of the damage and intended repairs. Any missing information is identified before work begins.

Is a site visit required?

No. Most estimates are prepared from client documentation. A site visit is available when measurements or photographs cannot be obtained.

Do you negotiate with insurance carriers?

No. El Dorado Claim Services provides estimating services only. Estimates are submitted to the carrier by the client or contractor. Policyholders seeking claim representation should retain a licensed public adjuster or an attorney.

Which price list is used?

The current Xactimate price list for the property's location, unless otherwise requested.

Can the estimate be imported into our Xactimate account?

Yes. An ESX file is included with every estimate.

What is your service area?

Desk estimates are available nationwide. Site visits are available throughout Northern California, Southern Oregon, and Northern Nevada.
